# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss technical decisions and evidence without personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be assessed in context, and enforcement will be proportionate to the behavior.

Report concerns privately to deps2ak@gmail.com, the public maintainer contact listed on npm. The project maintainer, DepsCian, handles reports and will avoid sharing identifying information unnecessarily.
